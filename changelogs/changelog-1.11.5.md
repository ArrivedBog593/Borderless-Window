# Borderless Window 1.11.5

*NeoForge · Fabric 1.21.1 · 2026-11-17*

> **One fix: the Screen Mode selector after F11.** In Minecraft's own video settings, a click on the selector right after pressing F11 could do nothing.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- Every setting stays as it was
- When updating, delete the 1.11.4 jar from your mods folder

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Screen Mode in Minecraft's video settings after F11.** With the video settings open (without Sodium), pressing F11 updated the selector, but the next click on it could do nothing until the screen was reopened. It now always switches. With Sodium, its page was not affected

## 📦 Dependencies

- Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.26 or newer, or with Fabric Loader 0.16.0 or newer
- Optional: Sodium 0.8.x on either loader, and Mod Menu on Fabric
