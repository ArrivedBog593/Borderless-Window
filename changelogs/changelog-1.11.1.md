# Borderless Window 1.11.1

*NeoForge · Fabric 26.3 · 2026-11-21*

> **A small safeguard for Alt+Tab in Fullscreen.** Nothing changes in normal use.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- Every setting stays as it was
- When updating, delete the 1.11.0 jar from your mods folder

## 🔧 Changes

- **Alt+Tab out of Fullscreen is now guarded against Windows putting the game back on screen.** On the other Minecraft versions, right after Alt+Tab at a lower Fullscreen Resolution, Windows could show the game again without the focus, and it stayed in front until clicked; their latest versions minimize it again. On 26.3 this was not seen in testing, but the same safeguard is now here: if the game is ever left on screen without the focus, it gets minimized again. Borderless and Windowed are not affected
- **Back from Alt+Tab, the Fullscreen Resolution is re-applied only once the game has the focus again**, not while it would still be in the background

## 📦 Dependencies

- Minecraft 26.3 with NeoForge 26.3.0.76 or newer, or with Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or newer
- Optional: Sodium 0.9.2 or newer on Fabric, Sodium 0.9.3 or newer on NeoForge, and Mod Menu on Fabric

En 26.3 no se vio al probar, pero la misma protección ya está aquí: si el juego llega a quedarse en pantalla sin el foco, se vuelve a minimizar. Sin bordes y Ventana no se ven afectados
- **Al volver de Alt+Tab, la Resolución de pantalla completa se vuelve a aplicar solo cuando el juego ya tiene el foco**, no mientras seguiría en segundo plano

## 📦 Dependencias

- Opcional: Sodium 0.9.2 o más nuevo en Fabric, Sodium 0.9.3 o más nuevo en NeoForge, y Mod Menu en Fabric
