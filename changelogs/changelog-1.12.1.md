# Borderless Window 1.12.1

*NeoForge · Fabric 1.21.11 · 2026-11-28*

> **A slider for falling blocks and TNT, and every entity in its place.** Wandering traders now follow Villagers and Golems, mannequins follow Armor Stands, and leash knots follow Frames.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- Every setting stays as it was. Armor Stands and Frames keep their values under their new names
- When updating, delete the 1.12.0 jar from your mods folder

## ✨ New Features

- **Falling Blocks and TNT** slider on the Distances page: falling sand, gravel, concrete powder and anvils, and lit TNT. Lower it for sand and gravel farms and TNT duplicators. Hidden TNT still explodes
- **Mannequins** now follow the Armor Stands slider, renamed **Armor Stands and Mannequins**
- **Leash knots** on fences now follow the Frames slider, renamed **Frames, Paintings and Leash Knots**

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Wandering traders followed Land Animals**, since Minecraft counts them as creatures. They now follow Villagers and Golems, as its tooltip already said

## 🔧 Changes

- New mod icon, in the mod lists and Sodium's settings

## 📦 Dependencies

- Minecraft 1.21.11 with NeoForge 21.11.42 or newer, or with Fabric Loader 0.17.3 or newer
- Optional: Sodium 0.8.x on either loader, and Mod Menu on Fabric
- Not compatible with Chloride
